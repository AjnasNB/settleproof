## Capture and editorial rules

- Replace provisional screenshots after the current UI build is stable. Never use an old capture to claim a newly fixed interaction passed.
- Keep source labels visible. Use tightly framed screenshots of the actual controls for readable captions and details; do not redraw the interface.
- Sandbox evidence is an integration gate, not a decorative placeholder. Parent requested a truthful current-build review video even if PayPal remains unavailable; explicitly show that limitation. The AI proof shot still waits for an actually accepted generated response. Do not present the review video as proof that every hackathon requirement is complete.
- Keep captions within safe margins and leave enough reading time. Screen content should remain visible behind captions, with no manufactured performance or adoption numbers.
- Use clean cuts and restrained emphasis. Do not animate a synthetic click or network response as though it were captured interaction footage.
- Before final render, run the HyperFrames workflow checks and inspect midpoints/cuts. Record sixteen distinct final video QA checks, not sixteen claimed rewrites of each frame.
- Public YouTube upload and hackathon submission remain with the parent/user. This agent performs no public action.
