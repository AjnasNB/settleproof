# Frozen source ledger

Every product image in assets/ is a screenshot of the actual SettleProof UI, captured through CUA on17October2026. These are edited screenshot sequences, not a continuous screen recording. CSS crops enlarge original pixels without generating or reconstructing the interface.

Payment cases are synthetic. AI-generated.png is a genuine accepted Workers AI explanation of the synthetic partial-capture case, observed by the parent and retained in ../../ai-ui-proof.json. No credentialed PayPal response or money movement is shown. sandbox-pending.png is the actual current Not configured status.

The first matched/unpaid source captures were rejected during frame review because their displayed states differed from their proposed captions. They were replaced with independently checked current UI screenshots before assembly. Unknown, source-record and setup states were checked as actual images as well.

Font: Geist1.8.1, package obtained from https://registry.npmjs.org/geist/-/geist-1.8.1.tgz . Local variable font with OFL text in assets/fonts/LICENSE-Geist.txt.

Animation runtime: GSAP3.14.2 frozen from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/gsap@3.14.2/dist/gsap.min.js . Original copyright/license header retained. No runtime external asset fetch is required by the composition.

No voice, music, SFX, generated imagery, customer data, private documents, credentials, personal payer details or paid media are included. Public upload and submission were not performed by this agent.
