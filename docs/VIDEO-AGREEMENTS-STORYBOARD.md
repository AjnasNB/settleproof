## Film direction

Use genuine current-build footage/screenshots, warm ivory/green product styling and existing licensed Geist fonts. Show a browser viewport or a readable crop, with a concise caption outside the UI. No reconstructed interface, generated screens, simulated cursor, invented provider values or sped-up clicks presented as real-time. Clean cuts between actual states are acceptable; show loading/latency as an honest cut rather than fabricating instant responses.

Target a calm English narration of approximately 280 words; captions below are exact short headlines, not dense transcripts. Narration is optional until actual audio is produced and checked. If the finished film is silent, keep enough readable evidence and captions to explain the complete journey; do not claim a voice track exists. Music is unnecessary. Any added audio requires documented rights and a proper mix.

Capture at source resolution appropriate to the final crop. Keep roles, version numbers, criteria, amounts, payment states and failure reasons readable at a normal 1080p view. Keep all capability links, browser fragments, account credentials, payer details and API secrets out of public frames. Frame the room title/role/actions while excluding invite-link input rows and the address bar. Do not blur a secret and then leave it readable in another frame or transition.
